Three gates. One graph. Zero guesses.

① Policy Gate — regulated client-confidential internal public

An unknown recipient, an unauthorized export, a payment without you: hard-blocked on the spot — the request never reaches the world. Every tier × action combination is decided by an explicit rule table; if a rule is missing, the gateway refuses to improvise.

② Fact Gate — every number carries receipts

Prices, award values, percentages and UENs in any draft are matched against your ledger and Singapore’s public records — each fact stored with its source_uri and date. A contradicted claim is pinned beside the record that disproves it: claimed S$1.2M → registry S$412,000.

③ Promise Gate — commitments with a birth date

Your terms change over time; your obligations don’t. The gate knows the price in effect on the day it is said: a stale S$1,200 pitch to a new prospect is held with corrected wording — and the same number is released to a client whose 2025 contract froze it. Different senders, one truth.
